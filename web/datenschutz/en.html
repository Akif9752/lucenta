In short

Lucenta has no server. Your answers, your result, your history, your check-ins and your profile never leave your device – except to your own iCloud if you choose to sign in with Apple (see below). There is no account with us, no advertising, no tracking and nothing passed to third parties.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

If you sign in with Apple, Lucenta also backs up your data in your own private iCloud (Apple CloudKit), end-to-end encrypted. We receive neither your name nor your email address, only a random identifier that stays on your device. Only you and your Apple Account can access this data, not us. You can sign out any time in Settings; “Delete sign-in and iCloud data” there also deletes the backup in your iCloud.

Apple Health (optional)

If you turn on the Health option in the settings, Lucenta adds the mood of each check-in to Apple Health on your device as a state of mind. Lucenta does not read any data from Health. None of it is transferred to us or to third parties or used for advertising. You can revoke the permission in Health at any time.

What is stored on your device

  • lucenta_result — your latest result (five numbers)
  • lucenta_history — up to twelve earlier results with dates
  • lucenta_facetten — the result of the in-depth test (29 facets), if you took it
  • lucenta_state — up to 800 check-ins: energy and mood with date and time of day, plus anything you add voluntarily (answers to the five questions, tags, a note)
  • lucenta_profile — name, user ID, chosen figure, colour, background, accessories and a picture you chose
  • lucenta_freunde — up to 30 saved friends with name, figure and code
  • lucenta_vergleich_frei — the codes of the people you compared with without Lucenta+
  • lucenta_progress — an interrupted test run
  • Settings for appearance, language, haptics, motion and reminders, and your Lucenta+ status

This information is kept in your device’s local storage. So that iOS does not lose it and the widget can show today’s status, Lucenta also keeps a copy in the app’s files on your device – and, if you sign in with Apple, encrypted in your own iCloud. It is never transferred to us or to third parties. You can delete it yourself at any time under Settings → Data & privacy → “Reset my data”, or by deleting Lucenta from your device.

Fonts

The Fraunces typeface is part of the app; nothing is downloaded when you open it, and there is no connection to Google Fonts or any other third party. Fraunces is used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. All other text uses your iPhone’s system font.

Your rights

You have the rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR), as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because we store no personal data about you, we can neither provide information about your content nor delete it — it exists only on your device.
